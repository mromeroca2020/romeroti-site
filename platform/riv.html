Romanoti Platform / RIV

Visualize infrastructure, trace connectivity, and guide technical operations.

RIV — Romanoti Infrastructure Visualizer — is the infrastructure visibility module of Romanoti Platform. It helps model racks, devices, topology paths, connectivity, power context, labels, and Smart Hands execution workflows.

Why this matters

Infrastructure is not only inventory — it is operational context.

Technical teams often need to understand where a device is located, which rack unit it occupies, what port is involved, what path the connection follows, which panels are part of the route, and what a Smart Hands technician must do on site. RIV is being developed to make that context visible and actionable.

Hidden connectivity

Source-to-destination paths are often spread across racks, panels, MDF points, backbone links, labels, and field notes.

Field execution risk

Smart Hands teams may need precise rack, RU, port, cable, optics, and validation instructions before touching production infrastructure.

Fragmented documentation

Infrastructure knowledge can live across spreadsheets, diagrams, tickets, and individual experience instead of one operational model.

RIV turns infrastructure visibility into operational guidance.

The current RIV pilot demonstrates topology visualization, selected object details, route intelligence, label visibility, port intelligence, Smart Hands previews, and operational reporting.
