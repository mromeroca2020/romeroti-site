Specialized operational backup support

This module is not an antivirus and it is not a general server administration tool. It is designed to help technical teams document, validate, trace, and support backup and restore operations with clear evidence and continuity-focused workflows.

01

Backup Validation

Track backup status, last successful jobs, failed jobs, scope, frequency, and validation notes.

02

Restore Support

Guide restore requests with requester, target system, recovery point, priority, and approval context.

03

Evidence & Traceability

Collect operational evidence, screenshots, notes, validation logs, and closure details for audit-ready reporting.

04

Continuity Readiness

Support continuity reviews by identifying backup gaps, recovery risks, pending validations, and next actions.
