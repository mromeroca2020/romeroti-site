Romanoti Backup Reality & Continuity Validation

Romanoti does not perform backups and does not replace backup platforms, vendors, MSPs, or internal IT teams. It acts as a proprietary validation layer that helps operators create execution orders, review routine backup evidence, validate restore readiness, identify gaps, and give business owners a clear view of whether their backup and recovery processes are trustworthy.

01

Validation Orders

Create guided operational orders to verify backup scope, recent job results, restore point availability, evidence, and validation notes.

02

Restore Test Orders

Guide restore validation activities with requester context, target system, recovery point, test result, evidence, and closure notes.

03

Routine Evidence Review

Review screenshots, job references, timestamps, logs, provider reports, operator notes, risks, and pending validation items.

04

Owner Continuity Report

Translate technical backup and restore evidence into a clear business-facing summary of reliability, gaps, pending actions, and continuity readiness.
