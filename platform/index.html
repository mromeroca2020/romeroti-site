How Romanoti Platform Works

Romanoti Platform is organized as a staged operating model: understand the environment, guide the next action, and connect the work to business execution.

RIV and NetPilot support the visibility layer: infrastructure mapping, discovery, diagnostics, and technical context.

→

→
